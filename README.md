ROBOTEC AG — Project Overview

Robotics + Agriculture + Web3 Technology

Welcome to the world of ROBOTEC AG, where Robotics, Agriculture, and Web3 Technology come together to present a new vision for the future of agriculture.

ROBOTEC AG aims to make farming smarter, more efficient, and technologically advanced through modern technology.

Today, we will explore the vision of ROBOTEC AG, the RMOMA Robotics Concept, and the digital ecosystem of RMOMA Coin.

1. ROBOTEC AG — A New Vision for Agriculture

ROBOTEC AG envisions integrating Robotics, Artificial Intelligence (AI), Computer Vision, and Blockchain Technology into the agricultural sector.

The proposed ecosystem includes Mobile Robotic Platforms, Precision Agriculture, Remote Monitoring, and Robotics-as-a-Service (RaaS).

Its objective is to improve access to modern robotic services for farmers and support greater operational efficiency in agricultural activities.

2. RMOMA Robotics Concept

RMOMA stands for Robotic Mobile Manipulator.

It is a robotics concept comprising four major technological components:

1. Mobile Base: Responsible for robotic movement, mobility, and navigation.

2. Robotic Manipulator: Designed to perform tasks using different tools and attachments.

3. Vision System: Includes cameras, crop observation capabilities, and object detection.

4. AI and Control System: Supports task planning, navigation intelligence, and operational decision-making.

ROBOTEC AG aims to integrate these technologies with agricultural services to contribute to the development of smart farming.

3. RMOMA Coin — Token Specifications

RMOMA Coin is presented as a digital token based on the BNB Smart Chain, using the BEP-20 token standard.

According to the project presentation, the proposed token specifications are:

- Token Name: RMOMA Coin
- Token Symbol: RMOMA
- Blockchain Network: BNB Smart Chain
- Token Standard: BEP-20
- Proposed Total Supply: 21,000,000 RMOMA
- Initial Pre-Mine Allocation: 3,000,000 RMOMA
- Future Mintable Allocation: 18,000,000 RMOMA
- Proposed Minting Period: Up to 7 years

The proposed token model connects minting with eligible staking deposits.

The model is designed around a maximum seven-year minting period, with a provision to burn remaining unminted tokens after the designated period, subject to the applicable protocol rules.

4. RMOMA Coin — Key Features

The project presentation highlights the following proposed features:

- Staking-Linked Minting
- Automated Liquidity Management
- Three Burning Mechanisms
- Controlled Token Distribution
- Long-Term Supply Management

According to the project presentation:

- 20% of eligible staking deposits is designated for liquidity management.
- 7.5% of each withdrawal is designated for liquidity management.
- 2.5% transaction burn is described for eligible transactions.
- 0.1% monthly burn is described for eligible pre-mined RMOMA balances, subject to applicable smart contract rules and available balances.
- A long-term supply-burning mechanism is also included in the proposed model.

The actual implementation and operation of these mechanisms depend on the deployed smart contract and its rules.

6. Robot Rental Share Program

ROBOTEC AG presents a Robot Rental Share Program with different daily share rates and durations based on direct referral conditions.

According to the project presentation, the proposed structure is:

Without Additional Direct Referrals

- Daily Rental Share: 0.5%
- Duration: 200 days

With 3 Direct Referrals

- Daily Rental Share: 0.75%
- Duration: 133 days

With 5 Direct Referrals

- Daily Rental Share: 1%
- Duration: 100 days

With 10 Direct Referrals

- Daily Rental Share: 2%
- Duration: 50 days

These figures represent the program structure described in the presentation and should not be interpreted as guaranteed earnings.
